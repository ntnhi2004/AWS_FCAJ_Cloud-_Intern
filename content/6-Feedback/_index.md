### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The working environment during the internship program was comfortable, friendly, and supportive. Team members were willing to help when I encountered difficulties during the learning and project development process, particularly with AWS configuration, Terraform, IAM, serverless deployment, and troubleshooting issues during hands-on activities.

The flexible working arrangement also allowed me to balance my university schedule with my internship responsibilities. However, I believe the program could organize more regular sharing or discussion sessions among interns to strengthen connections and provide opportunities for interns to share their experiences and technical problem-solving approaches.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors provided detailed guidance, especially on relatively complex topics such as AWS architecture design, VPC and networking, IAM, Terraform, serverless architecture, and AWS cost optimization.

What I particularly appreciated was that the mentors did not simply provide direct solutions. Instead, they encouraged me to analyze the problem, study the documentation, and develop my own solution. This approach helped me become more proactive when researching AWS services and troubleshooting issues during the CloudMenu project.

The Team Admin also provided good support regarding internship documentation, procedures, and questions related to the program.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship activities were highly relevant to my major in Computer Science and to the knowledge I have gained at university.

Through CloudMenu, I had the opportunity to apply knowledge of software engineering, databases, computer networking, and cloud computing to a practical system. In particular, working with the frontend, backend, database, and infrastructure helped me better understand how different components of a software system interact and operate in a cloud environment.

The internship also helped me recognize the difference between understanding a technology at a theoretical level and actually deploying, configuring, and operating that technology in a real system.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Participating in the internship program allowed me to expand my knowledge in several areas, particularly AWS Cloud. The main topics I had the opportunity to study and practice included:

- AWS architecture design and system layering.
- Amazon S3 and CloudFront for frontend hosting and content delivery.
- Amazon API Gateway and AWS Lambda for serverless backend development.
- Amazon DynamoDB for application data storage.
- AWS IAM and IAM Roles/Policies for access management.
- VPC, subnets, Internet Gateway, NAT Gateway, VPC Endpoints, and Security Groups.
- Terraform for infrastructure as code and infrastructure deployment.
- Amazon CloudWatch for log and metric monitoring.
- AWS cost analysis and optimization, particularly for development and testing environments.
- Potential extensions such as Amazon Cognito, AWS WAF, CI/CD, and advanced monitoring mechanisms.
- Technical documentation and system architecture diagram development.

In addition to technical knowledge, I also improved my soft skills, including teamwork, task management, progress reporting, communication, and technical presentation.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
I had a positive impression of the working culture throughout the program. Team members were supportive and respectful toward each other during the working process. When I encountered difficulties, I could discuss them with mentors or other team members and receive guidance instead of having to solve every issue completely on my own.

In particular, discussions about architecture and AWS service selection helped me understand the importance of maintaining consistent design decisions and sharing technical knowledge within a development team.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provided a relatively flexible schedule and the necessary learning materials, allowing interns to study and conduct research proactively.

For me, the greatest value of the program was the opportunity to work with AWS Cloud and a relatively complete system architecture, covering requirement analysis, architecture design, service selection, infrastructure deployment with Terraform, security analysis, and cost analysis.

This experience gave me a more practical perspective compared with learning technologies only through theoretical coursework.

### Additional Questions

- **What was I most satisfied with during the internship?**  
What I am most satisfied with is having the opportunity to independently research and design the CloudMenu architecture on AWS, rather than simply studying individual AWS services separately. Designing flows such as CloudFront → S3 and API Gateway → Lambda → DynamoDB, together with IAM, Terraform, VPC, and CloudWatch, helped me better understand how AWS services can be integrated to build a complete cloud-native system. This was a valuable practical experience that I had not had many opportunities to gain before joining the program.

- **What Could Be Improved?**  
The program could provide more advanced workshops on topics such as advanced serverless architecture, AWS Security, Infrastructure as Code, and CI/CD. In addition, more architecture review or technical sharing sessions among interns would provide opportunities to learn from different projects and approaches.

- **Would I recommend this internship to others? Why?**  
Yes. I would recommend the program to my friends because it provides interns with opportunities to work with AWS technologies in a practical environment, receive guidance when facing difficulties, and develop both technical and professional skills. In particular, for students interested in Cloud Computing, AWS, Software Engineering, or Cloud-Native Development, the program provides a suitable environment for turning theoretical knowledge into practical experience.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

My suggestions for improving the program in the future include:

- Organize additional advanced AWS workshops, particularly on serverless, security, networking, and Infrastructure as Code.
- Organize regular technical sharing and architecture review sessions among interns.
- Provide additional standard checklists or guidelines for processes such as - Terraform deployment, IAM management, security reviews, and AWS cost optimization.
- Organize more product demonstrations or sprint review sessions so interns can present their results and receive feedback.
- If advanced projects or further internship opportunities become available, I would be very interested in participating to further develop my knowledge of AWS Cloud, Cloud Architecture, and Cloud-Native Application Development.
