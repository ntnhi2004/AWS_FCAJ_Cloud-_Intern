### Week 2 Achievements

- Apply **IAM** principles to manage Users, Roles, and Policies.
- Implement selective access control and organize permissions appropriately.
- Understand the relationships between **Users, Groups, Roles, and Policies** in AWS.
- Successfully deploy, connect to, and manage **EC2 Instances** using the Free Tier.
- Perform basic EC2 operations through the **EC2 Console**.
- Understand the fundamentals of **VPC**, including Subnets, Route Tables, Internet Gateways, and NAT Gateways.
- Understand routing mechanisms and network segmentation within a VPC.
- Learn about **AWS Site-to-Site VPN** and its mechanism for connecting on-premises networks to a VPC.
- Explore **Amazon Bedrock**, Claude, Titan, and basic prompt engineering techniques.
- Strengthen **Python programming** skills through hands-on practice with AWS services.
- Gain an overview of **Compute, Networking, and AI/ML** services on AWS.
